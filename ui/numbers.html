The booking files hold — verified restaurant pages in all, and — kitchens in the graph carry a verified link on their dossier.

A link is kept only when

  • the booking page’s address names this restaurant, on a word boundary;
  • no leftover word points to a second name or another branch;
  • the page names San Francisco, or names no other city;
  • the dossier’s name is the exact name the page was verified for.

A same-named restaurant in another city fails the third check. No match, no button.

What this page does not count.

How many kitchens on record sit outside San Francisco is not given here. The record holds no location per kitchen, so that number cannot be read from it, and none is offered. The permit file places — linked kitchens in San Francisco; the rest are closed San Francisco kitchens, Bay Area kitchens and kitchens elsewhere, and the record cannot split them.

A new market needs three things from this repository, and no date is given because none has been measured: a source list (the reading list and the corpus queries), the permit file of each city so kitchens can be placed, and the star list for that city, matched to kitchen names. The extraction, the quote check, the citation gate and the identity rules carry over unchanged.

The record, in one file, for anyone who wants to check it.
